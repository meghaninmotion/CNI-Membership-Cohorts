# CNI-Membership-Cohorts
A prototype mapping of CNI membership continuity - through the lens of cohorts, or a group of organizations that joined in the same year

This visualization presents three separate years, the organizations that joined CNI within that year, and the tenure of each organization. Note that the dataset underlying this visualization is incomplete and some membership details, including some organizations, may not be represented. Instead this visualization is presented as starting point to explore variables that may have impacted continuity or disengagement over time. The organizations have been anonymized for this view.  

More details on the organizations can be found at https://www.cni.org/about-cni/membership/members.

This visualization was developed by pairing with Claude AI to develop html to represent the longitudinal tenure of members, and is presented for exploration. It can be further developed with your feedback and suggestions. Please share those thoughts - and report errors - as pull requests!
